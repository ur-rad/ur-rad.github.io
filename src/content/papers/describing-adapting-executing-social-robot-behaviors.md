title: "Describing, Adapting, and Executing Behaviors across Social Robot Platforms"
abstract: "Robot platforms provide various hardware and software capabilities, each unique combination resulting in vastly different interfaces, and requiring researchers and developers to focus their efforts on a single platform. The differences make it tedious and time consuming to implement social behaviors on another platform. To ease the development of social behaviors and simplify working with multiple social robot platforms, we propose using a novel language to facilitate LLM-based behavior generation and a modular middleware bridging the language to a robot’s API. Through a demonstration and two ablation experiments, we show that our approach can describe a range of social behaviors, adapt behaviors to a platform, and successfully execute them on two different social robot platforms. By relieving some of the challenges in implementing social behaviors across multiple platforms, our language and technology provide an approach to advancing end user development, teleoperation authoring, and autonomous social robots."
