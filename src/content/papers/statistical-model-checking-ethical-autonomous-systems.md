title: "Statistical Model Checking of Ethical Autonomous System Under Uncertainty"
abstract: "Artificial intelligence based systems are omnipresent in our daily lives. It is imperative that the decision making processes in these systems are ethical. The evaluation of ethical decision making becomes challenging when system models are constructed with imprecise data and often, do not address environmental fluctuations during their operations. A tractable formalism, based on interval discrete-time Markov chain to incorporate uncertainty, is constructed. Deontic logic based ethical rules are encoded in the formalism. Statistical model checking is performed on a prototype to demonstrate the computational feasibility and address epistemic uncertainty in the model. Temporal logic queries is posed on the model abstraction and the effectiveness of the results are discussed."
