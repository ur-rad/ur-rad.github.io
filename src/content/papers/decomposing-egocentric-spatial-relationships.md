title: "Decomposing Egocentric Spatial Relationships with Intermediacy and Imagery"
abstract: "Natural language communication about spatial relationships and orientations is a crucial aspect of human-robot interaction. This work continues efforts to represent natural language expressions about spatial relationships in qualitative non-standard coordinate systems while reaping the benefits of primitive decomposition. In this short paper, we propose cognitive representations of egocentric spatial relationships based on a “betweenness” spatial primitive and imagery-constructed region objects. We demonstrate how the scheme can represent left and right relationships between pairs of objects and “on the left/right” relationships between an object and an observer."
