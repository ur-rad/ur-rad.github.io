Dr. Stephanie Lukin is a Senior Computer Scientist at the U.S. DEVCOM Army Research Laboratory at the Los Angeles regional site (ARL-West). She holds a Ph.D. and M.S. in Computer Science from the University of California Santa Cruz, and interned at Xerox PARC, Microsoft Research, and Google before joining ARL in 2017. Dr. Lukin specializes in narrative intelligence and situational awareness, examining multi-modal human-robot dialogue and embodied intelligence to create shared understanding for more effective collaboration.
